# Practice 1: Service definition and requirements

Define a satellite-enabled service for a customer who needs connectivity outside terrestrial coverage and in one high-demand urban zone.

## Expected student tasks

- Describe the service and identify the target users.
- Classify the scenario.
- Translate the service into coverage, latency, availability, and capacity requirements.
- Separate mandatory and desirable requirements.

## What to revisit
NTN use cases, scenarios, service models, QoS targets, KPIs, and requirements.
